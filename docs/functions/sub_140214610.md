## Purpose

Runs the betrayal announcement of a unit and clears its betrayal flag.

## Behavior

- V: plays the sound 0xA6 through sub_1400F4BCC, calls BattleVoice_AssignPatterns for the unit, waits frames, then clears bit 7 (0x80) of the unit's dword +504 (a bit-reset instruction at 0x1402147FA).
- I: the panel helper it calls (sub_14014A870) plays the panel state 'ShowGafgarionBetrayal'.
