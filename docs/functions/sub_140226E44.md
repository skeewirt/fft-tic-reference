## Purpose

Refreshes the battle unit panel when an item is selected, checking the item's record category and then its weapon sub-record to decide what the panel shows.

## Behavior

- V: It reads an item id from a battle structure, masks it to nine bits for the record lookup, and compares a record field against a category value.
- V: The weapon sub-record lookup that follows uses the same word without the nine-bit mask, so the two lookups can disagree for an id of 512 or more.
- V: A word equal to 255 is treated as no item and skips the update.
- V: When both lookups agree it writes a word into a global panel field.
- V: Five direct callers reach it and it calls eight helpers, including the record and weapon accessors and a scene refresh.
- I: The inconsistency between the masked and unmasked lookups is harmless in stock because valid ids stay below 256, and it becomes visible as soon as they do not.
