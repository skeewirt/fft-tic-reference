# sub_140226E44

- **Address:** `0x140226E44`
- **Area:** [Battle](../areas/battle.md)
- **Prototype:** `void __fastcall sub_140226E44(void)`
- **Callers:** `BattleTask_Slot13Entry`, `sub_140229D84`, `BattleTask_ActorForecastPanel`, `BattleTask_TargetForecastPanel`, `sub_14022A19C`
- **Callees:** `sub_140226CA0`, `Item_IsValidId`, `Skillset_BuildAbilityList`, `sub_14030ED60`, `Item_GetRecord`, `Item_GetWeaponRecord`

Confidence tags: **V** decoded, **M** measured, **I** inferred, **?** unknown.

## Purpose

Refills the two shared stat blocks that the battle unit panels read, one from the actor slot and one from the target
slot of the current preview pair. `sub_140226CA0` fills the common part (level, HP/MP, CT, EXP, team, turn-order
position, live-unit count); this function then builds the stat, equipment and ability tail of the actor block. The
alternate status panel's number table points straight at these blocks, and the detailed stats screen's number tables
point at the stat part.

## Behavior

- V: when `word_1407DCFAA` (the target slot of the preview pair) is 20 or less, it takes
  `g_PreviewUnitTable + 512 * slot` and fills the block at `0x140783320` through `sub_140226CA0`. A larger slot
  leaves that block untouched.
- V: when `HIWORD(dword_1407DCF98)` (the actor slot) is 20 or less, it fills the block at `0x1407832A0` the same
  way, then runs the actor-only tail below. A larger actor slot skips that fill and the tail (the target block was already filled before this test).
- V: `sub_140226CA0` writes the common part of a block: level at +0x00, the 0..3 team/auto code at +0x02, the
  unit's turn-order position plus one at +0x04, a live-unit count at +0x06, EXP at +0x08, current and maximum HP at
  +0x0C/+0x10, current and maximum MP at +0x12/+0x16, CT (unit byte +0x41) at +0x18 and the constant 100 at +0x1C.
  HP max and MP max are forced to 1 when the unit has 0. CT becomes 100 when the unit's byte +0x1B8 is set.
- V: the team code is 1 when bits 0x30 of unit byte +0x1EE are not zero, 2 when the whole 0x38 mask is clear, and
  3 when unit byte +0x1EC is non-zero; otherwise 0.
- V: the +0x04 word is the result of `BTurn_ForecastIndex`, plus one when that result is not negative. The helper
  rebuilds the 40-entry turn list and returns the unit's index in it, or one of the negative codes -1, -2 and -3.
- V: the +0x06 word is a count over the 21 unit slots: a slot contributes when `SceneObject_FindBySlot` finds a live
  scene object, the slot maps to a preview index, and that index has a matching preview entry. It is a count of live
  preview units, not the number of occupied battle slots.
- V: the actor tail copies the job id (unit +0x03), Brave (+0x2B) and Faith (+0x2D) into `word_1407DCF92`,
  `word_1407DCF94` and `word_1407DCF96`, the zodiac nibble (word +0x08 >> 12) into the low word of
  `dword_1407DCF98`, and the first 16 bytes of the name string (+0x14C) into `xmmword_1407DC498`.
- V: the same tail copies the unit's stats into the 64-byte block at `0x1407832E0`: move (+0x42) at +0x00, the byte
  +0x40 at +0x02, jump (+0x43) at +0x04, the two weapon powers (+0x44, +0x45) at +0x06/+0x08, the two weapon evades
  (+0x46, +0x47) at +0x0A/+0x0C, and a 0/1 marker at +0x0E that records which tail branch ran.
- V: `0x1407832F0` gets the unit byte +0x3E at +0x02, the byte +0x4B at +0x04, the larger of bytes +0x49/+0x4A at +0x06,
  the byte +0x48 at +0x08, the unit byte +0x3F at +0x0C and a zero word at +0x0E. I: +0x3E and +0x3F are derived attack values, not base PA (+0x38) and MA (+0x39); the stats screen labels them At and Atmagic (rests on those labels and on the base PA/MA offsets seen in play).
- V: `0x140783300` starts with the larger of bytes +0x4D/+0x4E at +0x00 and the byte +0x4C at +0x02.
- V: the detailed stats screen reads these words. Its number tables at `0x140808B30`, `0x140808B90` and
  `0x140808C10` point at them under the names Move, Jump, Wt, Weppowr, Weppowl, Wppwdfr, Wppwdfl, At, Def, Ar, Av,
  Atmagic, Defmgc, Armagic and Avmagic. I: `Wt` is the +0x40 byte, the unit's CT gain per tick (base Speed is +0x3A, seen in play), and `At` and `Atmagic` are the +0x3E and +0x3F words.
- V: when the unit's flags byte +0x06 has bit 0x20 clear, it fills five words at `0x140783304`..`0x14078330D`
  with the item ids of the first hand (+0x20, or the shield at +0x22 when the first is 0xFF or 0x105 and above), the
  second hand (+0x24, or +0x26 by the same rule), the head word (+0x1A), the body word (+0x1C) and the accessory
  (+0x1E). Each of the five becomes -1 when `Item_IsValidId` rejects it.
- V: the tail at `0x14078330E` depends on unit byte +0x12. When `(byte + 0x50)` taken as a byte is above 0x2F, it
  writes unit byte +0x12 at block +0x0E, unit byte +0x13 at +0x10, the unit words at +0x14 and +0x16 at block +0x12
  and +0x14, and the unit word at +0x18 at block +0x16.
- V: otherwise (byte +0x12 in the 0xB0..0xDF window) it builds the unit's ability list with
  `Skillset_BuildAbilityList(byte +0x12, 1)` and writes five ids into the same +0x0E..+0x17 space, dropping zeros and
  compacting the list. It also clears the fourth id (block +0x14) when `sub_14030ED60` returns 0, which it calls through `g_TextCommandSlot`.
- V: after either tail branch, any of the five words (+0x0E..+0x17) that is still 0 becomes -1.
- V: last, the weapon lookup runs only when exactly one of the two hand ids at `0x140783304` and `0x140783306` is -1;
  with both hands holding an item nothing is looked up and the word stays 0. It looks up the one that is not -1 (an id of 0
  is skipped). When the item record's byte +3 is negative, which marks a weapon, it reads the weapon record and sets the word
  at `0x1407832F0` to 1 if the weapon's byte +1 has bit 0, or if the unit's byte +0x9A has bit 1 and the weapon's
  byte +1 has bit 2; otherwise the word stays 0.
- V: the function is inline. It pushes no scene task, waits no frame and returns directly to its caller.
- I: the 64-byte block is the source record of the detailed stats screen and the common part is what the status
  panels draw; the equipment and ability tail must feed a panel that was not traced.
- ?: what unit byte +0x12 is beyond the 0xB0..0xDF window that selects the skillset list; what the 0/1 word at
  `0x1407832F0+0x00` marks; who reads the equipment and ability tail (the target-side forecast task reads two words of
  it, the marker at `0x1407DCFBE` and the word at `0x1407DCFE2`, but their consumer is not found).

## Notes

Whole body read (968 bytes) plus `sub_140226CA0`, and the copies checked against the disassembly. The unit field names
come from `UnitWorkRecord` and from play (level +0x29, EXP +0x28, HP +0x30/+0x32, MP +0x34/+0x36, CT +0x41, PA +0x38,
MA +0x39, Speed +0x3A, CT gain +0x40, move +0x42, jump +0x43, equipment +0x1A..+0x26, evades +0x48..+0x4E). The item lookup is one branch
inside this wider stat-block fill, not the whole function. The earlier version described this as an item-selection
refresh only. The stats-screen names are inline text in the number-table records and are the game's own labels.
