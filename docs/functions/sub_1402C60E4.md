## Purpose

Reconciles a unit's equipment words with the inventory: every equipped item is counted, duplicates beyond the cap are sold, and the unit's slots are cleared afterwards.

## Behavior

- V: It walks the 54 party units and the five hand words of each one.
- V: A word equal to 255 is skipped as empty; anything else is tested against the 261 bound before use.
- V: For an accepted id it asks the owned-and-equipped counter whether the party already has 100 or more, and sells the excess through the record price when it does.
- V: Otherwise it increments the inventory count of that id by one.
- V: After each unit it writes 255 into a per-unit byte slot in a 600-byte-stride array and calls a helper that finishes the unit's equipment pass.
- V: It carries four separate 255 sentinel loads and one 255 byte store, because each pass over the slots re-establishes the empty value.
- I: The per-unit byte slot is some kind of equipment-changed or equipped-count marker, but the field it belongs to was not pinned.
