## Purpose

Sells the equipment a unit owns beyond the 99-per-item cap, the equip-side twin of the inventory overflow pass.

## Behavior

- V: The unit index argument is accepted only up to 20, which matches the 21 battle unit records rather than the 54 party units.
- V: It walks the unit's five equipment slots and skips a slot holding 255.
- V: An id above the 261 bound is skipped as well.
- V: For a valid id it asks the owned-and-equipped counter for the total; at 100 or more it resolves the record and sells the excess.
- V: It calls four helpers and has two direct callers.
- I: The 21-unit limit and the battle record layout say this runs on the battle copy, not the roster copy.
