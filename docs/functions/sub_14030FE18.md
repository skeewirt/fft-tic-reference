## Purpose

Answers whether a unit can take its turn: a short predicate over four status flags and the display-list gate.

## Behavior

- V: It returns 1 when the unit's second byte is 0xFF.
- V: While the UI core dword at +0x347C is not zero it returns 1 for +0x61 & 0x40, and for +5 & 4 together with +0x61 & 0x20.
- V: Otherwise it returns 1 for +0x61 & 0x60.
- V: It also returns 1 for +0x62 & 0x81 and for +0x1B4 & 0x40, and 0 when none of the tests fire.
