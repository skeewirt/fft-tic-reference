## Purpose

Builds a unit's working stats from its job row and its equipment words, one of the two paths that turn equipped items into stats.

## Behavior

- V: It sign-extends the unit index from the second argument and keeps the fourth argument as a pointer to the output work record.
- V: It reads the five equipment words of the unit and filters them against 255, so an empty slot contributes nothing.
- V: The job row is read through the first argument and supplies the base stats before the equipment contributions are added.
- V: One direct caller reaches it; it calls ten helpers, including the job and record accessors.
- I: The equipment filter being 255 rather than a range means an id above 255 would be treated as a real item here, unlike the readers that also test the 261 bound.
