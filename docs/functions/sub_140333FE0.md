Confidence tags: **V** decoded, **M** measured, **I** inferred, **?** unknown.

## Purpose

Refreshes the equip preview: it zeroes or fills the five candidate words, rebuilds the stat block, and compares
each unit hand word with the block to mark changed rows.

## Behavior

- V: When `a1` is set it clears five words starting at `g_EquipCandidates` (0x14033401E), including the fifth
  at 0x141876C6C; otherwise it fills them with `sub_1402875A4`.
- V: It calls `sub_140287354` with `g_EquipSet`, the record, the unit's +0x54 and the candidates, then computes
  the two-handed word with `sub_140284998`.
- V: It reads the block's +0x10 word (`xmmword_141876EA0`, 0x1403340E2) and compares each of the five unit words
  with the record's block entry; a mismatch or a zero entry sets the changed byte in the 5-row structure.
- V: The comparison uses the raw unit word (`*v11 != ...`), so a bit-15 word counts as changed even when its id
  equals the candidate.
- V: It does not write a flag; the candidate fill and the two-handed word are the only writes.
- I: The block's +0x10 word is the two-handed value in this path, not a record field.
