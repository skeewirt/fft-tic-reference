## Purpose

Opens the Outfitter's equipment-change state: it backs up the selected unit's four editable hand words, builds
the five candidate words from the shop list, refreshes the panel block, and on the leave message applies the
candidates to the unit and charges their prices.

## Behavior

- V: On the first pass it saves the four words at unit +0x54..+0x5A to an 8-byte stack slot with
  `sub_1402D7584(&v26,0,0)` (0x1402D5D75), builds the candidates with `sub_140287D9C` (0x1402D5D84), stores
  the fifth candidate `word_141876C6C` from unit +0x5C (0x1402D5DA3), writes the saved four words back
  (0x1402D5DAF) and calls `sub_14028728C` to compute the stat block (0x1402D5DBE).
- V: It writes the two-handed word at 0x141876C50 with `sub_140284998` (0x1402D5DE0) and sets the screen
  flags `byte_143CDA8BB`, `byte_140782B8F` and `dword_14184A9B8`.
- V: On the leave message (the wait returns 0) it walks four slots. The candidate is loaded signed
  (a signed load at 0x1402D5ED3); a zero candidate or one with bit 15 set (a bit-15 test of the candidate at 0x1402D5EEB)
  goes through `sub_1402D6464` on the current unit word and adds the candidate's masked price to
  `dword_143D1AC68`.
- V: For a plain candidate it tests the current unit word's bit 15 with a zero test and a nonzero branch
  (0x1402D5EFA/0x1402D5EFD): bit 15 set means the old price is subtracted and the override cleared; bit 15
  clear means the override is cleared only when it equals the candidate.
- V: It stores the candidate word at unit +0x54..+0x5A (0x1402D5F69), so a bit-15 candidate becomes the unit
  word, then calls `PartyUnit_ReloadEquipment` (0x1402D5F88) and closes the screen state.
- V: It tests only bit 15 (`0xFFFF8000`), not bit 14; a bit-14 candidate would be stored. No stock builder
  produces one on this path.
- I: The two `sub_1402D7584` calls are a no-op pair: the first copies the words to the stack and substitutes
  overrides, the second restores the untouched stack copy.
