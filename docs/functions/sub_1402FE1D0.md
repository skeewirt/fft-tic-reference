## Purpose

A battle unit panel builder that also handles the small per-slot status icons next to each equipment row.

## Behavior

- V: It masks equipment ids to nine bits before the record lookup and treats 255 as empty.
- V: Several byte-sized fields of the record and of the unit state are widened into word globals for the draw calls.
- V: It calls seven helpers and has three direct callers.
- I: The byte-to-word widening around the draw is the panel's own storage, not an item id; the id path itself is the mask-and-record pair.
