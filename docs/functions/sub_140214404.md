## Purpose

Runs the withdrawal announcement of a unit and clears its withdrawal flag.

## Behavior

- V: plays the sound 0xA5 through sub_1400F4BCC, fills the unit info with UnitInfo_FillFromBattleUnit, waits frames, then clears bit 6 (0x40) of the unit's dword +504.
- I: the panel helper it calls (sub_14014A768) plays the panel state 'ShowWithdrawal' (that string is referenced by it and its siblings, not by this function).
