## Purpose

Classifies an item into the equipment slot group it belongs to, and writes the id and the group into a two-dword structure.

## Behavior

- V: it stores the id at `[out]` and the group at `[out+8]`.
- V: an item of category 1 (the second weapon range) takes group 5 without reading the record.
- V: otherwise the record's slot-flag byte at +3 decides: bit 0x80 (a sign test, not an immediate) or 0x40 gives group 1, bits 0x30 give group 2, bit 0x08 gives group 4, and anything else falls through to group 5.
- V: a record with the high bit of the flag byte set is also group 1.
- I: the groups are the equipment slots the battle and menu code uses: hand, head/body, accessory, and an "other" bucket.
- ?: which group number corresponds to which named slot; only the numbers were read.
