Confidence tags: **V** decoded, **M** measured, **I** inferred, **?** unknown.

## Purpose

Validates and commits the chosen ability command, then puts the unit into its charge pose.

## Behavior

- V: the unit's ability word at +0x17A is masked with 0x1FF before the aux lookup, so only the low nine bits reach Ability_GetAuxRecord.
- V: the aux record and the action record are read for the chosen ability.
- V: the charge motion is set and the command is committed with BattleAction_ConfirmCommand.
- V: the range helper sub_14027FFE8 is called, so the target ring is prepared as part of the confirm.
- I: this is the confirm step of the ability page: it is where an ability id becomes a queued action.
- ?: the 0x1FF mask caps ids at 511.
- V (2026-11-10): the result of BattleRange_ApplyShape on the unit's request block is stored in the unit's int32 at +0x194 (unit work record), and the commit follows it: -1 calls BattleAction_ConfirmCommand(0) and drops the confirm; 0 or 1 sets battle state 25 (0x19, the target confirmation); 2 starts the charge motion (BattleAct_SetChargeMotion); 3 or more does nothing.
- V: in the branch taken when the unit's byte +0x154 has bit 0x08, the ability word (+0x17A) is cut to 9 bits before Ability_GetAuxRecord and the command byte at +0x179 is looked up in g_CommandKind: kind 10, or kind 0 with the extra record's byte +12 & 0x7F non-zero, triggers sub_1400D6DAC.
