## Purpose

Fills a 72-byte local record from the selected party unit's words, including the five equipment words copied
raw into +0x1A..+0x1E and +0x20..+0x26.

## Behavior

- V: It reads +0x54 and +0x56 and passes each raw word to `Item_GetRecord` (0x14028564D, 0x14028567E); the
  category decides the slot pair.
- V: It copies +0x58, +0x5A and +0x5C raw to +0x1A, +0x1C and +0x1E (0x140285699..0x1402856D0).
- V: It calls `sub_140287398` on the unit's hand pointer to fill the stat bytes at +0x44..+0x47.
- V: No mask and no flag test; a bit-15 word would be copied and looked up raw.
- I: This is the party-record variant of the battle copy; it is used when the unit has no battle-table row.
