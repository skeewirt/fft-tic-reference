# sub_14033FB84

- **Address:** `0x14033FB84`
- **Area:** [Items and shops](../areas/items.md)
- **Prototype:** `void sub_14033FB84(void)`
- **Callers:** Game_MainLoop
- **Callees:** memset

Confidence tags: **V** decoded, **M** measured, **I** inferred, **?** unknown.

## Purpose

Re-initialises a block of small per-entity records and clears a 0xA0-byte area beside them; it is called from the main loop.

## Behavior

- V: Five records of 0x38 bytes are walked in a loop, with 0xFFFF written into their word fields (the all-ones word held in rdi after ORing it with -1) and zero into their dword fields.
- V: Four 16-byte constants are copied over the head of the same block.
- V: memset clears 0xA0 bytes starting at 0x1411A7030.
- V: The lowest address it writes is 0x1411A7028.
- V: Two callers, both Game_MainLoop.
