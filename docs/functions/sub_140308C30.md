## Purpose

The formula handler for the HP-restoring consumables: it derives the amount from the used item's chemist row and
hands the result to the shared healing step.

## Behavior

- V: it takes no arguments and reads the battle context.
- V: it rebuilds the item id from the byte at `0x1407B077E`; the writer stores the id's low byte plus `0x10` there, so adding `0xF0` in this body completes the round trip (the `0x10` is not an operand here).
- V: only the low byte of the id survives that round trip; an item whose low byte is `0xF0` or above rebuilds as
  one of 240..255 instead of itself.
- V: it calls Item_GetChemistRecord for the rebuilt id and reads the row's second byte, the amount field.
- V: it multiplies that byte by ten and stores the product as a 16-bit amount in the result block at +6.
- V: it ends with a tail call to sub_140306378 instead of returning normally.
- I: the HP amount is the field at +6, unlike the MP formula which writes +0xC, because the three stock users of
  formula 72 are Potion, High Potion and X-Potion.
- I: the ten-times scale stores the amount in tens in the item table (Potion's 3 becomes 30).
- ?: what sub_140306378 does with the amount after the tail call.
