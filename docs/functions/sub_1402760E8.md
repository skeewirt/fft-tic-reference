## Purpose

The end of a unit build: loads the unit's equipment, applies the equipment attributes and the move and jump bonuses, then sets the name key.

## Behavior

- V: calls Unit_LoadEquipmentAndEvade, then Unit_ApplyEquipment(work, 1, row), then Unit_ApplyMoveJumpBonuses(work, 0), then returns UnitWork_SetNameKey(work, row).
- V: the second argument is the OverrideEntryData row of the unit's entry (null when the unit has none); it is passed on to Unit_ApplyEquipment, whose Unit_ComputeStats pass adds the row's speed_bonus word to the final Speed byte (work +58), and to UnitWork_SetNameKey (chara_name column).
