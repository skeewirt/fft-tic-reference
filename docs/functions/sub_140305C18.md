## Purpose

Applies the three multiplicative modifiers that follow the attack scale: a 4/3 step, a 3/2 step and a final halving.

## Behavior

- V: It tests three independent flags on the context: +0x99 & 0x10, +0x9A & 0x20 and +0x9B & 1.
- V: The first flag multiplies the working value by 4/3 (shift left by two, then divide by three).
- V: The second flag, only when the item category at record+5 is zero, multiplies the value by 3/2.
- V: The third flag multiplies by 3/2 again and stores the halved result at 0x1407B0770.
- V: Eight callers, six of them formulas (StealEXP, Monk averaged, Pummel, Purification, Chakra, Revive, Rush).
- V: correction to the bullets above: the third step multiplies A by 3/2 (4/3 for +0x99 bit 0x10, 3/2 for +0x9A bit 0x20 when the item category is 0, 3/2 for +0x9B bit 0x01); there is no halving. Formula_PhysMods repeats the same steps inline.
