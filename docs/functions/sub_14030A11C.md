## Purpose

Finishes the result block of one resolved action: either drops the whole result when the acting unit's own state forbids it, or runs the per-formula post hook, clamps the numbers to 999 and decides whether a follow-up result is needed.

## Behavior

- V: tests three status bits of the acting unit (g_BattleActingUnit; the dword's flags are tested as bytes +0, +1 and +3 of 0x14186B028 and the later masks sit in registers): +0x61 bit 0x20 (KO; it counts only when bit 0x20 of dword 0x14186B028 is clear or the unit also has +0x61 bit 0x10), +0x62 bit 0x80 (with bit 0x8000 of the dword clear) and +0x64 bit 0x01 (with bit 0x1000000 clear); when one holds it removes the unit's slot byte (unit +0x1BC) from the 16-byte list at 0x14186B030, resets both result records with Battle_ResultReset (g_BattleActionBlock and qword_14186AF60), clears the flag bits it set and leaves byte +2 of the block at 8.
- V: otherwise, when runHook is non-zero and the gate g_ShopModeGate is non-zero, it hands the block's words +6 and +10 to sub_140309FB8 and writes them back, then runs the per-formula hook: row = g_PostBattleCalc + 8 + 32 * (formula - 1) for a formula byte (g_FormulaBlock +0x18) below 107; when the row's argument qword (+8) is non-zero it calls the row's function (+0) with that argument and a pointer to a copy of the row's second half (the row's +0x10 and +0x18 qwords).
- V: then it clamps the block's words +6, +8, +10 and +12 (signed) to 999; when the HP word (+6) reaches the acting unit's current HP (+0x30) it also clears the g_FormulaBlock flag byte (+0x20, bit 0x80) with block word +18 bit 0x4000 and the dword at 0x1407B0768 with word +18 bit 0x200; and, when the block's byte +39 and byte 0 are non-zero outside the battle-state gate, a per-side word of qword_14186B020 is set and bit 0x08 of byte 0x1407B0799 is set, it sets byte +2 = 10 and word +18 = 0x8000 and calls sub_14030A43C.
- V: the hook rows exist for 12 formula ids only; formula ids above 106 skip the hook by the bound (no overflow).
