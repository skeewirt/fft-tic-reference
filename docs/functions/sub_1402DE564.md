## Purpose

A battle unit panel builder, the twin of the one at sub_1402C4D1C: it fills a unit's equipment rows from the unit's item slots.

## Behavior

- V: It masks each equipment id to nine bits before the record lookup.
- V: A slot holding 255 draws no row.
- V: The record's category byte chooses the icon and the sub-record for the row.
- V: It calls seven helpers and has five direct callers, more than its twin.
- I: The two builders differ in layout only; the item handling is the same mask-and-record pattern.
