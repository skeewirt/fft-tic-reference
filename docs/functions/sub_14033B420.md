## Purpose

A battle unit panel builder that fills the unit's equipment rows and the small slot markers next to them.

## Behavior

- V: It masks each equipment id to nine bits before the record lookup and treats 255 as empty.
- V: The record's category byte chooses the row's icon and sub-record.
- V: It also copies the unit's equipment words into a panel-side buffer, widening each one as it goes.
- V: It calls seven helpers and has one direct caller.
- I: The copied block is the roster record's equipment block; the battle panel keeps its own copy of it.
