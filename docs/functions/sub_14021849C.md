Confidence tags: **V** decoded, **M** measured, **I** inferred, **?** unknown.

## Purpose

Validates a resume payload's header and copies its summary fields into the auto slot.

## Behavior

- V: the payload pointer and size come from the manager's group object through the accessor sub_1400C43D8.
- V: the magic must be FFTI, the version must be 0x7A, the kind must be below 2, and the self-check must hold: the first dword times the structured size equals the dword at +0x10.
- V: the summary fields are copied into g_SaveAutoSlot: leader name, tag 0xFE, job, level, two place bytes, map id, leader slot, cleared and new-game flags, progress, play time and timestamp (0xFE is the record's fixed summary tag, not an immediate here).
- V: the payload's kind byte is not imported; the body is left to the materialiser.
- V: a payload that fails any check is refused and nothing is copied.
- I: this is the header half of a load: it lets the Continue card be built before any 668 KB body is touched.
