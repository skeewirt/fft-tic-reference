# sub_14030CF38

- **Address:** `0x14030CF38`

Confidence tags: **V** decoded, **M** measured, **I** inferred, **?** unknown.

## Purpose

Adds an item's sale value to the gil total: a quarter of the item price, added to scene variable 44 and clamped.

## Behavior

- V: It does nothing when the no-consume dword at 0x14186AF80 is set, or when the item record's +5 byte has bits 0x30.
- V: When the caller passes a second item id it resolves that record and adds a quarter of its price (price >> 2) to the amount.
- V: It reads scene variable 44 through SceneVar_Read, adds the amount, clamps the sum to 99,999,999 and writes it back with SceneVar_Write.
- V: While the sale runs it also stores the amount at +0x3528 of the object at 0x143CD9DC0.
- V: Two callers: Battle_ApplyActionResult and Inventory_ReturnItem.
