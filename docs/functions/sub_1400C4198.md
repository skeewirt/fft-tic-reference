Confidence tags: **V** decoded, **M** measured, **I** inferred, **?** unknown.

## Purpose

Prepares and queues one named resume entry: formats the entry name from a part and a point, then hands the group to the packer.

## Behavior

- V: the name is produced by the name builder from the part index (main, world, attack, fturn) and the point index.
- V: it is called eight times by the flush, once per group-and-point combination the ring writes.
- V: it does not touch the payload itself; the group's buffer is read by the packer.
- V: the flag argument is passed straight through to the packer, so a caller can force the direct queue path.
- I: eight calls in one flush explain why the autosave container holds up to twelve resume entries after a full rotation.
- ?: what the part index maps to beyond the four names the builder knows.
