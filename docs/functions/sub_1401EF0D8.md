## Purpose

Per-frame routine of the deployment screen; at its first step it sets the capacity from the deployment areas.

## Behavior

- V: in the start step it reads scene variable 52 and sets the capacity (+64) to the count of area 0 (Deployment_SelectArea(0)) plus that of area 1 when variable 52 is 2.
- V (seen live): in a random battle with one area of count 5 the capacity read 5.
