Confidence tags: **V** decoded, **M** measured, **I** inferred, **?** unknown.

## Purpose

The Outfitter commit strip: it removes the session bits from every party equipment word, returns each stored
original to the inventory, and clears the override array.

## Behavior

- V: It walks `g_PartyCount` units and five words per unit (unit +0x54..+0x5C) with the parallel override
  entry at 0x14184A680.
- V: It compares each unit word with 0x4000 (the 0x4000 constant at 0x1402D6617, a comparison with 0x4000 at 0x1402D6621,
  an unsigned branch at 0x1402D6624). A word below 0x4000 is left untouched.
- V: A word at or above 0x4000 has its low ten bits extracted with a 0x3FF mask (0x1402D6632) and the
  override compared with them (0x1402D6634); the unit word is then masked with 0x3FF (a 0x3FF mask of the unit word at
  0x1402D6641), which clears both flag bits.
- V: It returns the override word to the inventory with `Inventory_AddCount(override,1)` (0x1402D6650) and
  clears the override (0x1402D6655), for every slot.
- I: Because the mask is 0x3FF, a word with bit 14 set is stripped the same way as a bit-15 word; bits 10..13
  would also be cleared, but no stock writer sets them.
