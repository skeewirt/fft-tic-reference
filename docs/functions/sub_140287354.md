## Purpose

Clears the equip block and recomputes it from the record, the unit words and the candidate words through
`sub_140287118`.

## Behavior

- V: It clears the block's first qword and the words at +0x08..+0x0C, +0x12 and +0x1C (0x14028737B..0x140287389).
- V: It passes the block, the record, the unit word pointer, the candidate pointer, the job word and the
  selected unit to `sub_140287118` (0x14028738D).
- V: It does not read the hand words itself; the callee masks them with 0x3FF.
- I: This is the one-line wrapper used by every equip screen refresh to keep the stat block in sync.
