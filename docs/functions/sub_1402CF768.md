## Purpose

The reverse of the slot-record builder: it takes the work copy and writes its sections back into live state, including the
ten item order lists.

## Behavior

- V: passes an incoming register through to its first helper together with the value 1, so it does take at least one argument.
- V: then calls one routine with five section pointers taken from globals.
- I: that routine restores the world/campaign half of the record, resting on its name and the section pointers.
- V: writes the five item order lists back into their live arrays one at a time, and the whole equipment block in one move,
  in the opposite direction from the builder.
- V: the six copies have the same sizes as on the save side: 282, 60, 76, 68, 42 and 1010 bytes.
- V: the live arrays it writes are the same ones the manual save unpacker writes, so both restore paths agree.
- I: the three callers are load-related paths (their names are not informative), which fits a restore.
- ?: the prototype; only the first argument is forwarded in the head.
