# sub_1402D6464

- **Address:** `0x1402D6464`
- **Area:** [Unit data](../areas/unit-data.md)

- **Callers:** `sub_1402D5CC4`, `sub_1402D6914`, `sub_1402D7280`
- **Callees:** `Item_GetRecord`

Confidence tags: **V** decoded, **M** measured, **I** inferred, **?** unknown.

## Purpose

Clears one party equipment slot and either refunds a pending purchase or preserves the original word in the
per-unit override array.

## Behavior

- V: It reads the selected unit's word at +0x54 + 2*slot and tests it against zero (0x1402D648C).
- V: A word at or above 0x8000 (a comparison with 0x8000 at 0x1402D6490 and an unsigned branch at 0x1402D6496) is a session-pending
  word: it is masked with 0x3FF (0x1402D64AA), priced, and the price is subtracted from `dword_143D1AC68`
  (0x1402D64BB). No override is stored.
- V: A word below 0x8000 is stored whole in `g_UnitEquipOverride` at 0x14184A680 (0x1402D649F), so bit 14 can
  be preserved there.
- V: It then clears the unit word at 0x1402D64C1.
- I: The two branches are "undo a pending purchase" and "remember the original item"; the second is what the
  cancel path restores.
- V: the pending word is reduced to an id by AND 0x3FF (mask loaded at 0x1402D64AA) before the price lookup; a mask that keeps bit 15 prices the record past the item block (behavior OutfitterPreviewReadsRecordPastItemBlockWithSessionFlag).
