## Purpose

Rebuilds the equipment order list for the current slot after the player picks a sort: it sorts the candidate list by the
chosen field, then copies the sorted result into the saved order list of that slot.

## Behavior

- V: reads the chosen sort selector through a UI helper and the current slot through another.
- V: builds the candidate list into the shared display list, sorts it with the field sorter, and fills the rows.
- V: then builds a second copy into a 146-word stack buffer, sorts that copy too, and copies it into the equipment order
  list of the slot.
- V: the copy into the saved list stops only on the 0xFFFF terminator (the -1 held in r12d after ORing it with -1, compared as a 16-bit word); it has no length check of its own.
- V: the destination is chosen from the four-entry equipment list table; slots zero and one share the first entry.
- V: it returns without a value.
- I: this is the "apply sort" action of the equipment screen, as opposed to the item menu's sort, which writes the other
  list family.
- ?: whether the unbounded copy can be reached with stock data (the list capacities leave at most two spare words).
