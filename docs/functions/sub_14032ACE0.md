## Purpose

Builds the portrait draw records for one or two save units in a layout chosen by *mode, and returns the first unit's portrait code byte (sub_14032AB3C) or -1.

## Behavior

- V: first sets the position words of four 136-byte draw records at recs (from qword_1408F3298).
- V: *mode 0: returns sub_14032AB3C(*indexA, recs, .., outColour) and nothing else (the path Unit_GetSpriteSetId uses).
- V: *mode 1: the same for *indexB, then *mode += 1.
- V: *mode 2 and 3: two records (indexB, then indexA at recs + 136) with their positions copied into the palette words of pal; *mode 4 to 9: four records with per-mode offsets from the byte tables at 0x1406839D8 and 0x1406838E4 (mode 4 also sets bit 15 of 32 palette words first); *mode 10 or more returns -1. Then *mode += dword_14078D578.
