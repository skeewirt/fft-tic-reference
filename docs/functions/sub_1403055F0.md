## Purpose

Seeds the attack and defence bytes of the formula working area from the attacker's hands and accessory before a physical formula runs.

## Behavior

- V: It writes 100 to 0x1407B077F, zero to 0x1407B0783 and copies the context byte at +0x4B into 0x1407B0784.
- V: The context flag at +6 & 0x20 skips the hand reads entirely.
- V: Otherwise it resolves the item ids at +0x1E, +0x20, +0x22 and +0x26 through Item_GetRecord and the per-slot accessors, and copies weapon and armour bytes into 0x1407B0781 and 0x1407B0782.
- V: A live flag tested through sub_14030DD1C decides whether the block bit at item+3 means 'this hand is used'.
- V: Two callers: Formula_01_NormalAttack and sub_140306C90.
