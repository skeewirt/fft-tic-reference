## Purpose

Decides which save lists the load screen may build: it probes the enhanced and classic containers, falls back to the autosave area, and enables the matching list modes.

## Behavior

- V: it queries the manager's enhanced and classic existence slots, choosing between them by a global word; a false answer falls through to the area existence probe.
- V: when any probe answers true it sets its "has a save" byte; otherwise the byte is clear and the screen shows no lists.
- V: it clears the screen's dword at `+0xD8`, copies the manager's byte at `+0x378` into `+0xE5` and the probe result into `+0xE4`.
- V: it then calls the per-list builder for mode 3 and 5 unconditionally, and for modes 0, 1, 2 and 4 only when the corresponding flag is set.
- V: three call sites, all in the save/load screen's own state machine.
- I: the modes are the screen's tabs: classic list, enhanced list, autosave/continue and the empty states.
- ?: the exact mode-to-tab mapping; only the conditional structure was read.
