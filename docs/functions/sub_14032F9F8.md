## Purpose

The equip detail panel: it reads the five hand words at 0x141876EB4 and a second five-word array at
0x141876EBE, and draws both rows without extracting the top two bits.

## Behavior

- V: It reads the header words at 0x141876E9E and 0x141876EA0 (0x14032FB70, 0x14032FB93) to choose the text
  bank and layout.
- V: The first icon loop walks five words at 0x141876EB4 (0x14032FF05) and masks each id with 0x1FF; it calls
  `sub_1402E1604` with the whole word, so the icon wrapper masks again.
- V: The second loop walks five words at 0x141876EBE (0x14032FCEC) and treats 0xFFFF as empty; it builds the
  text with 0x7000/0xB000 plus the word.
- V: It never does a shift right by 12/a mask of 0xC; the top two bits are not consumed here.
- I: This is the "detail" variant of the panel family; the flag-aware variants are sub_14032EDB8 and
  sub_140399A64.
