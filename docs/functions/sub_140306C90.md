## Purpose

Second half of the physical attack setup: it applies the attacker's state modifiers after the hands are read and returns whether the action may continue.

## Behavior

- V: It calls sub_1403055F0 first, so the hand values are always refreshed before the modifiers.
- V: Attacker +0x99 & 1 zeroes the dword at 0x1407B0781 (the four hand bytes).
- V: Attacker +0x62 & 0x30 halves the working byte at 0x1407B077F, and +0x63 & 0x10 zeroes the dword at 0x1407B0781.
- V: It calls sub_140305700 and sub_14030573C, then for weapon categories 11 and 12 reads scene variables 35 and 36 and scales the byte at 0x1407B077F by three quarters when variable 36 is 1.
- V: It returns true only when the byte it finally tests is zero.
- V: Twenty-three callers, nearly all physical formulas.
- V: this is the physical gate of the formula blocks (proposed name Formula_PhysGate): after the evade bytes are prepared it calls Formula_EvadeRoll and returns 1 when R+0 is 0 (the hit failed), else 0; 23 formula functions call it directly.
- V: for item category 11 or 12 (record +5 of the item word at g_FormulaBlock +0x0A) it scales the baseline byte by 3/4 when SceneVar 36 is 1 and by 3/4 again when SceneVar 35 is 3 or 4.
