## Purpose

Fills an item descriptor from the record table and the sub-record that matches the item's category.

## Behavior

- V: It zeroes two words of the output structure before filling it.
- V: The id argument is masked to ten bits and passed to the item record accessor.
- V: The record's kind byte then selects the weapon, shield, armor, accessory or consumable sub-record, and that sub-record supplies the rest of the descriptor.
- V: It calls six helpers and has two direct callers.
- I: The ten-bit mask is the category-table width, not the record width; both lookups would accept a wider id.
