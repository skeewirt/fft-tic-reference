## Purpose

Materialises a resume payload: it copies the body back into the live buffers, restores the control words and inflates the image page.

## Behavior

- V: it copies the entry into the same scratch the writer used and branches on the payload's kind at +0xC.
- V: kind 0 copies 40,164 bytes to the checkpoint copy at 0x142E80450; kind 1 copies 668,112 bytes to the battle snapshot buffer at 0x1408167A0.
- V: the saved battle control words (0x142E7FC78, 0x142E7FB0C and 0x142E7FB10) are restored from the payload.
- V: the YOX page is inflated into 0x142FF9F90 with the pack inflater, and sub_14025FC38 lays out its rows.
- V: when called with part 2 it also imports the second 40,164-byte work copy for the checkpoint.
- I: it is the only consumer of the payload, so every Continue, battle resume and checkpoint load ends here.
