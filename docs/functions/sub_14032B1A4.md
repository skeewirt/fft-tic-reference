Confidence tags: **V** decoded, **M** measured, **I** inferred, **?** unknown.

## Purpose

A battle/result panel that copies the 0x141876E90 block into the 0x14078DDD0 render area and draws the unit's
equipment summary.

## Behavior

- V: It copies `xmmword_141876E90`/`xmmword_141876EA0`/`ymmword_141876EB0` to 0x14078DDD0/0x14078DDE0/
  0x14078DDF0 with four 16-byte moves (0x14032BFEF, 0x14032BFE0, 0x14032C004, 0x14032C019). The block's hand words
  at +0x24 travel with the copy.
- V: It reads the record's +4 word and forms a cell value with a sign shift right by 15 (0x14032BFF6) and a mask by 0xBFE plus
  2. That is bit 15 of the **record's +4 field**, not of a hand word.
- V: It draws six equipment rows from the record/block and uses `sub_1402EC3C8`/`sub_1402EC9D0` for each.
- V: It does not test bits 14..15 of any hand word; the only bit-15 test is the record +4 field above.
- I: The copy is the reason the block's flags reach the render area; the panel itself uses only the ids.
