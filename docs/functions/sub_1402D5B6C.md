## Purpose

The Outfitter cancel/restore: for every party unit and every one of its five equipment words, it puts the
override word back when the live word is flagged or when an override exists, then clears the override.

## Behavior

- V: It walks `g_PartyCount` units and five words per unit (unit +0x54..+0x5C) and the parallel
  `g_UnitEquipOverride` entry at 0x14184A680.
- V: The replacement condition is `unit_word >= 0x4000` or `override_word != 0`; the constant 0x4000 is loaded
  at 0x1402D5B9E and compared at 0x1402D5BA6. So a word with bit 14 or bit 15 set is always replaced, even
  when the override is zero.
- V: The replacement copies the whole 16-bit override word (a zero-extending read of the override word at 0x1402D5BB2, store at
  0x1402D5BB5), then clears the override (a store that clears the override at 0x1402D5BBA).
- I: The function makes the session disappear: a pending word is dropped and the original word (or empty) is
  restored. It is the cancel counterpart of the commit strip.

## Notes

- V: It does not mask with 0x3FF: the restore is a whole-word copy, so a bit-14 override survives if one was
  stored.
- V: It is called from the cancel branches of `sub_1402D6914` and `sub_1402D6E14`; the two call sites are the
  only direct calls.
