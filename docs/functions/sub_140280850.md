## Purpose

Marks the units that satisfy the two Arithmeticks mask abilities of an action; returns the number marked, -1 when invalid.

## Behavior

- V: requires the action's two ability words in 414..421; kind5 bytes are read via the virtual base: v3 = g_AbilityKind5_vbase[word1], v4 = g_AbilityKind5_vbase[word2] (0x1402808CC, 0x1402808E4).
- V: accepts only when `(v3 & 0xF0) != 0`, `(v4 & 0x0F) != 0`, `(v3 & 0x0F) == 0` and `(v4 & 0xF0) == 0` (tests `& 0xF0` at 0x1402808CC and `& 0x0F` at 0x1402808E4).
- V: for each of 21 units a value v8 (chosen by v3 bits 0x40 / 0x20 from two unit bytes, otherwise a derived byte, or the +33 byte when v3 is negative) is tested against v4: bit 8 = membership in table byte_14067E1D0 (54 entries), bit 4 = multiple of 5, bit 2 = (v8 & 3) == 0, bit 1 = multiple of 3.
- V: matching units set flag 0x80 on their tile record byte 5 at 0x140D8DCB0 + 8*tile and are counted.
