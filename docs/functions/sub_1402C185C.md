## Purpose

Builds a unit's detail panel (job line, icon, equipment, ability names) from a party record handed over in three copied parts.

## Behavior

- V: the record arrives in parts: `header` = the party record's +34..+47 (its +2 is the job word +36, its +10 the save slot +44), `middle` = from +48, `block` = from +112 (unlock mask, learned bits, levels, JP). Every writer of the header buffer qword_1407B2758 fills it from +0x22, +0x2A and +0x2E (sub_1402BBBCC, JobScreen_RefreshAfterChange, PartyUnit_ReloadEquipment); sub_1403783F0 writes a header of its own.
- V: the job line goes through JobRecord_FromJobId with a key whose pointers are block + 77 (levels), + 90 (JP) and + 136 (total JP), the job word from the header; then the icon and generic flag (JobRecord_FillIconAndGenericFlag; markers 0x80 / 0x81 use job 74, 0x82 the header's job), five equipment words from the middle part, five ability names (sub_1400FB8E4), and sub_1402C17F4(header, middle, block).
- V: callers: sub_1402C19EC (passes its own three parts on), sub_140333FE0 (the preview record at 0x141876C10 with the header buffer), sub_14036BBFC (the Fitting screen: stack copies of party record 54).
- V: a stack copy of the block belongs to no record.
