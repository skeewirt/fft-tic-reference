## Purpose

Chooses the item a chest unit gives to the unit that steps on it.

## Behavior

- V: returns 255 at once while g_BattleSimulating is non-zero.
- V: it reads the seven u16 values at the dead unit's record +26..+39 and counts those that Item_IsValidId accepts.
- V: with at least one valid id it picks one of them with the game's xorshift generator (state xmmword_1408E5FC8), the index being (random 15 bits, masked with 0x7FFF, x count) / 0x8000, the divisor reached as the shift by 15.
- V: with none it builds a random id near 240..253 from the same generator (the range is read from the immediates 240, 245 and the 13-wide scaling; the exact set was not enumerated).
- I: the seven words are the dead unit's equipment slots, since the span +26..+39 covers the right and left hand offsets used elsewhere (+0x20, +0x24; those offsets are not in this body); so a chest gives back one piece of what the unit wore.
