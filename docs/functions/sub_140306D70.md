Confidence tags: **V** decoded, **M** measured, **I** inferred, **?** unknown.

## Purpose

Magic-side counterpart of the physical setup: it seeds the magic working bytes from the caster's hands and accessory.

## Behavior

- V: It writes 100 to 0x1407B077F and zeroes the dword at 0x1407B0781 at the top.
- V: Caster +6 & 0x20 skips the hand reads; otherwise it copies byte +1 of the records for the item ids at +0x1E, +0x22 and +0x26 into 0x1407B0781, 0x1407B0782 and 0x1407B0783 (the per-slot accessor is chosen by the item's flag bits 8 and 0x40).
- V: It applies the same +0x63 & 0x10 zeroing of the dword at 0x1407B0781 as the physical setup.
- V: It calls sub_140305700, sub_14030573C and sub_14030588C, then returns (byte == 0) like its physical twin.
- V: Twenty-two callers, all magic formulas (Black Magick, Gravity, Faith status, Death, the drains, Iaido, Geomancy and others).
- V: this is the magic gate of the formula blocks (proposed name Formula_MagicGate): the baseline byte is set to 100, only the accessory and the two shield evade bytes are filled (the dword at 0x1407B0781 is zeroed first, so the fourth evade byte stays 0), then Formula_EvadeRoll runs; it returns 1 when R+0 is 0, else 0.
- V: the accessory is read when item record byte +3 has bit 0x08, a shield when its record byte +3 has bit 0x40; 23 formula functions call it directly.
