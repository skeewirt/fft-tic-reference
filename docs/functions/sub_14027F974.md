## Purpose

Marks the tiles of an action's area (the range marks) for a request; ability 220 gets four extra directional marks.

## Behavior

- V: copies the 20-byte request; its word at +2 is the ability id and the action row is fetched with AbilityAction_GetRecord.
- V: when the ability id is exactly 220, after the common marking it calls sub_14027F684 four times with the arguments 2, 0, 6 and 4: its own area in four directions.
- I: the rest marks the weapon reach, the diamond spread and flags target tiles through the BattleRange helpers (the callee list); only ability 220 gets the four directional marks (the test is on the exact id).
