## Purpose

Restores a list's cursor when the list is built again: it reads the list's saved entry (row, scroll, word) from the table at word_143760800 and puts the cursor back on the row that holds the saved word.

## Behavior

- V: the saved entry is three words at word_143760800 + 6 * slot: the row, the scroll position and the word that row held; the row goes to the first output and the scroll to the second.
- V: it counts the 0xFFFF terminators in the list's rows before the saved row; when the word at the saved row equals the saved word and no terminator came before it, the saved row and scroll stay.
- V: otherwise it scans the list from row 0 to the first 0xFFFF and stops at the first word that, masked with 0x3FF, equals the saved word: that row is the result. With no match both outputs are 0 (the top of the list).
- V: the saved word and the word at the saved row are read sign-extended (movsx); the scanned words are read zero-extended.
