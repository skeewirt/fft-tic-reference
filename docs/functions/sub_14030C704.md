## Purpose

Builds a four-byte battle-result entry for a unit: the two halves of a name id, the level and the job level, after applying the unit's action result.

## Behavior

- V: It returns -1 when the unit's second byte is 0xFF, the empty-slot marker.
- V: It copies the unit id byte at +0x1BC into the result field at 0x14186AF9C, sets the dword at 0x14186AFF4 to 1, and calls Battle_ApplyActionResult with the id.
- V: Out bytes 0 and 1 come from unit +0x1E8 and +0x1E9, the two halves of a name id.
- V: Out byte 2 is the unit level (+0x29) but only when the flag at 0x1407B07B6 is set.
- V: Out byte 3 is a job level nibble taken from the per-job byte array at +0xE4, halved according to the parity of Job_IdToIndex's result.
- V: One caller, sub_14020435C.
