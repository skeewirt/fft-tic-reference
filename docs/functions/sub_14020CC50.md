# sub_14020CC50

- **Address:** `0x14020CC50`
- **Area:** [Battle](../areas/battle.md)
- **Prototype:** `? sub_14020CC50(int slot)`
- **Callers:** The action resolution step (the impact of a resolved action)
- **Callees:** Effect_AbilityHasNoEffect, sub_1402178C0 (voice), sub_1402059AC, sub_140236468

Confidence tags: **V** decoded, **M** measured, **I** inferred, **?** unknown.

## Purpose

The hit sequence of an action: it picks the impact animation and voice from the effect word and starts the notification.

## Behavior

- V: it reads the motion byte (the second byte) of the ability animation row at 0x140672DA1, one of the eight consumers of that table.
- V: the effect word is special-cased by equality: 148 is the stone projectile, 0 and 512 mean no ability, and 0x1A6..0x1C5 has its own range (the end is 0x1A6+0x1F: the code subtracts 0x1A6 and compares the difference with 0x1F).
- V: the no-effect classification is asked from Effect_AbilityHasNoEffect before the animation is picked.
- V: the ability voice row is read through the voice helper, so the impact sound follows the same word.
- V: the end-of-action notification task is started from here.
- I: this is the impact step, after the formula has produced a result and before the banner retires.
- ?: what the 0x1A6..0x1C5 range means (its content is not decoded).
