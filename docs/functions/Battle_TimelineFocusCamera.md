## Purpose

One pass of the battle timeline's camera-focus step. It reads the shared battle step word `dword_140D3A86C` and runs one of four steps: place the timeline on the acting unit, wait for the panel's first transition, hold the camera while action 0xDB is active and preview the focused row's range, then wait for the panel's second transition and dispatch the queued battle state. It has no task id of its own and returns to the battle frame update.
