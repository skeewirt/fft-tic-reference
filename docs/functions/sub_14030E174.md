## Purpose

Per-kind list builder: it dispatches on the menu selector to the item list, the throw list, the Arithmeticks list and four more per-command builders.

## Behavior

- V: The selector is compared through a chain of subtractions, giving cases 0..15.
- V: Selector 1 calls Unit_ListItemCommandItems (0x14030E297) and selector 2 calls Unit_ListThrowableItems (0x14030E28A).
- V: Other selectors call Unit_ListArithmeticksAbilities, sub_14030E368, sub_14030EA38, sub_14030EB44, sub_14030EEF0 and sub_14030EFE4.
- V: The unit argument is turned into a 512-byte record index (shift left by nine) and bit 1 of the record's byte +3 is tested at 0x141853D43 + index*512; when it is set, selectors other than 0, 5, 6 and 8 return 0 early.
- V: One caller: sub_14030E010.
