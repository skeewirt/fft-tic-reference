# sub_14028728C

- **Address:** `0x14028728C`
- **Area:** [Unit data](../areas/unit-data.md)
- **Prototype:** `__int64 __fastcall sub_14028728C(__int64 block, __int64 unit, _WORD *unit_words, _WORD *candidates)`
- **Callers:** `sub_1402D5CC4`, `sub_1402D6914`, `sub_140333974`
- **Callees:** `sub_140286DDC`, `sub_140286E7C`

Confidence tags: **V** decoded, **M** measured, **I** inferred, **?** unknown.

## Purpose

Computes the equip block's stat deltas from the unit's live words and the candidate array, comparing each pair
masked to ten bits.

## Behavior

- V: It clears the block's first 0x24 bytes (0x1402872C1..0x1402872D7) and then loops five times.
- V: It reads the unit word and the candidate word, masks both with 0x3FF (0x1402872EE/0x1402872F6), calls
  `sub_140286DDC` with the pair (0x1402872FA) and accumulates the difference into the block with
  `sub_140286E7C` (0x14028730E).
- V: It accumulates two words at 0x1407B27D4 and 0x1407B27CE (0x140287323/0x14028732A), which are the record's
  attack/defence mirrors.
- V: It never writes the candidate or hand words; the outputs are the stat fields only.
- I: Because both sides are masked, a bit-15 unit word and a plain candidate compare by id, which is what
  makes the preview show the change rather than the flag.
