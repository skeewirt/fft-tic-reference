## Purpose

The battle-side flag-aware equip panel. It draws the five hand words of the battle copy at 0x141D3B880, a
64-byte copy of a battle record's +0x30..+0x6F region.

## Behavior

- V: It takes its carrier from `xmmword_141D3B890` (0x141D3B890) and its five hand words from
  0x141D3B8A4 (0x141D3B8A4); the header word it tests for zero is 0x141D3B88E (0x141D3B88E).
- V: The hand loop walks five words; it masks the id with 0x1FF (0x140399E0C), skips 0xFF and values at or
  above 0x105, and calls the item text/icon path for the rest.
- V: Bits 14..15 are extracted with a shift right by 12 (0x140399E24) and a mask of 0xC (0x140399E29) and stored in
  `dword_140D40978` (0x140399E35).
- V: The icon loop passes the whole word to `ItemIcon_Set`; that callee masks the id itself.
- V: The task waits one frame per iteration through `SceneTask_WaitFrame` and ends through
  `SceneTask_Terminate`; it is registered in the battle task manager (`off_140783088`), not in the field one.
- I: The 0x141D3B880 block is the battle counterpart of the field block 0x141876E90: same 0x40-byte size, same
  +0x24 hand offset, and the same 0x30..0x6F source slice.
- ?: the battle record `sub_14033E394` copies and whether its hand words can still carry a session flag.
