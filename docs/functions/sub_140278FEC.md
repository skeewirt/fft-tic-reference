## Purpose

Inventory count plus the copies worn by roster units and by the battle units.

## Behavior

- V: byte_1411A7C00[id] plus, for each used roster entry (54 x 600 B) not present as a battle unit, its 7 equipment words (+14) equal to the id; plus, for each battle unit record (21 x 512 B at 0x141853CE6 - 3) that counts, its 7 equipment words (+20).
