## Purpose

Sets or clears the countdown of a timed status on a unit record.

## Behavior

- V: status 2 sets unit +7 to 0xFF when (unit +5 & 4) or (unit +6 & 9), else to 3.
- V: statuses 24..39 only (the range check at 0x140278D10 subtracts 0x18 from the status and skips results above 0x0F, unsigned): with `clear` it zeroes unit +78 + status; otherwise it copies status entry byte 3 into unit +102 + (status - 24) (the address 0x140788FE3, entry 0 byte 3, is loaded at 0x140278D46 and indexed as that address + 16*status).
- V: status 39 uses slot 15 and returns -1 when unit +117 is already non-zero.
- V: returns 0 otherwise.
- I: byte 3 is the initial countdown of the status.
