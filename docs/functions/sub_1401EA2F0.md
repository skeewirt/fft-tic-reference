## Purpose

The objective camera tour of a battle: visits each unit marked as an objective target, an attack target or a boss, holding the camera on it for 120 frames with a sound.

## Behavior

- V: walks the 21 battle units; for the three marks it calls sub_1400F4BCC(.., 0x96) (the sound) and then waits 120 frames with SceneTask_WaitFrame.
- V: the first test is BattleObjective_ShowsTarget on the unit's flag dword +504 (bit 2, only while the objective asks for targets); the other two tests are bits 3 and 4 of the same dword.
- V: called by the event interpreter (EventScript_Run).
