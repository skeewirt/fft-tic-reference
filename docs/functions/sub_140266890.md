Confidence tags: **V** decoded, **M** measured, **I** inferred, **?** unknown.

## Purpose

Copies a classic item-icon tile state into the unit's icon sprite record, so the unit's item overlay uses that tile.

## Behavior

- V: takes the unit object and the item id.
- V: calls ItemIcon_Set into a stack icon state.
- V: copies the state's packed word to the sprite record at +6.
- V: copies the state's width and height to sprite record +0x10 and +0x11.
- V: copies the tile x and y to sprite record +0x12 and +0x13.
- V: computes the two draw offsets as (16 minus size) divided by two minus 8 and stores them at +0xE and +0xF.
- V: the sprite record is reached through the unit at +0x368.
- V: three direct call sites: two in sub_14020435C and one in BattleAnim_SelectMotion.
- I: the record is the unit's small icon/overlay quad rather than the body sprite, because it is 16 by 16 and is set from an item
  id.
