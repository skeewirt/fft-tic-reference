## Purpose

A two-row equip panel variant: it reads five display words at carrier +0x2E, not the five hand words, and draws
them in two groups.

## Behavior

- V: Task 10 takes the carrier 0x141876E90 and the words at 0x141876EBE/0x141876EC2 (0x14032F495/0x14032F482);
  other tasks take 0x141876C4E/0x141876C6E/0x141876C72 (0x14032F4DA/0x14032F4D3/0x14032F4C0).
- V: It draws two words from the +0x2E array and three from +0x32 (the 2+3 split at 0x14032F4D3/0x14032F4C0),
  so the five display words are 0x141876C6E..0x141876C76 / 0x141876EBE..0x141876EC6.
- V: It tests the header word at the carrier's +0x0E for zero (0x14032F4DA) to choose the text bank and the
  row layout.
- V: It does not extract bits 14..15 of any item word and does not read the +0x24 hand words; this panel is
  not one of the two flag-aware variants.
- I: The +0x2E display array is never written by any instruction in the .text census; the panel may be a
  disabled feature.
