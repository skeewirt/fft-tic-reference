# sub_14028531C

- **Address:** `0x14028531C`
- **Area:** [Battle](../areas/battle.md)
- **Prototype:** `char sub_14028531C(void)`
- **Callers:** not in the exported set; a battle-unit builder
- **Callees:** `Item_GetRecord`, `Unit_ReloadFromJobRow`, `sub_140287398`

Confidence tags: **V** decoded, **M** measured, **I** inferred, **?** unknown.

## Purpose

Copies the selected party unit's five equipment words into a battle unit record and mirrors the two hand words
into the save row, all raw.

## Behavior

- V: It finds the battle-table row for the selected unit's save index (0x1402853A0..0x1402853B4), clears the
  two hand pairs to 0x00FF00FF (0x1402853B9, 0x1402853E6) and reads the party words at +0x54 and +0x56.
- V: Each non-zero word is passed raw to `Item_GetRecord` (0x1402853CA, 0x1402853F7); the category byte +5
  decides whether it goes to the second or first word of the pair (0x1402853D5/0x1402853DC).
- V: The words +0x58, +0x5A and +0x5C are copied raw to battle +0x1A, +0x1C and +0x1E
  (0x140285415..0x140285444).
- V: It copies the battle hand pair back to the save row (0x14028546D..0x14028547D) and calls
  `sub_140287398` on the unit's +0x54 for the stat bytes.
- V: There is no 0x3FF mask and no bit-15 test: a flagged party word would reach the battle unit and the save
  row as a full 16-bit word, and the `Item_GetRecord` call would index past the table.
- I: This is a battle-start copy; the party words are assumed committed by the time it runs.
