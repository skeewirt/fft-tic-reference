## Purpose

Runs the guest-join announcement panel of a unit and clears its guest-join flag.

## Behavior

- V: plays the state names 'Guest01to02' and 'ShowGuest02StatusJoin' (the strings are referenced by this function and three panel helpers) and fills the panel with UnitInfo_FillFromBattleUnit.
- V: clears bit 5 (0x20) of the unit's flag dword +504 (at 0x14013F7F3).
- V: started from EventPreviewUnits_RunFrames for a unit whose bit 5 (OverrideEntryData guest_join_panel) is set.
