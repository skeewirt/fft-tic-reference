## Notes

V: the five equipment words of a party unit are at +0x54, +0x56, +0x58, +0x5A and +0x5C. During an Outfitter session bit 15 marks a session change and the price is in dword_143D1AC68; the commit handler sub_1402D65D4 strips it before sub_140285A6C writes the save.
